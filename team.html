Frequently asked questions

Who leads HiringGo?
HiringGo is led by founder and director Gagandeep Kaur alongside chief architect Ashutosh Taiwal, supported by a core team covering HR, recruitment, marketing, accounts, and business development.
How do I contact a specific team or department?
For hiring requirements, email hello@hiringgo.com. For job applications or resumes, email jobs@hiringgo.com. You can also reach the wider team through our Contact Us page.
Is HiringGo hiring for internal roles?
We’re always open to meeting strong recruiters, HR professionals, and operators. Send your resume to jobs@hiringgo.com and our team will review it.
Where is the HiringGo team based?
Our registered office is in Gurugram, and our team supports clients and candidates across 17+ cities in India.

Understanding Client Requirements

2

3

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does “hire in less than 24 hours” mean?
Our recruiters begin sourcing as soon as we understand your requirement, and can typically share an initial shortlist of relevant, screened candidates within 24 hours. Full time-to-hire still depends on the role’s seniority and how quickly interviews are scheduled.
What engagement models does HiringGo offer?
Permanent hiring, contract staffing, executive search, and outsourced HR/recruitment process support — choose the model that fits your team’s budget and timeline, and switch as your needs change.
Which cities does HiringGo operate in?
Our registered office is in Gurugram, and our recruitment team supports clients and candidates across 17+ cities in India, including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
How do I get started?
Email hello@hiringgo.com or visit our Contact Us page with your requirement, and our team will respond within one hour.
I’m a candidate — how do I apply for roles?
Send your updated resume to jobs@hiringgo.com or browse our job description templates to see the roles we typically recruit for.

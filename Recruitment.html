Our Recruitment Process

STEP 01

Understanding Client Requirements

HiringGo starts by fully understanding the client’s needs, ensuring an accurate understanding of their staffing requirements including skill set, experience level and cultural fit within the IT sector.

STEP 02

A Team of Recruiters Get Into Action

Our dedicated team of experienced IT recruiters leap into action, leveraging their industry expertise to identify and connect with top talent through proactive sourcing and personalized outreach.

STEP 03

AI-Based Candidate Evaluation Software

HiringGo uses advanced AI technology for candidate assessment — analyzing resumes, evaluating skills and assessing compatibility for an efficient, fair shortlisting process tailored to client specifications.

STEP 04

Client Assessment of Shortlisted Candidates

Before finalizing, HiringGo ensures clients participate in the selection process — presenting an organized list of shortlisted candidates to assess, interview and give feedback on.

STEP 05

Streamlined Documentation & Onboarding

To speed up the hiring journey, HiringGo streamlines documentation and onboarding, handling paperwork carefully and ensuring a fast turnaround while minimizing administrative hassle.

STEP 06

Rolling Out the Job Offer

Once approved by the client, HiringGo makes job offers, facilitates conversations, resolves questions, and ensures a smooth transition for candidates who accept.
